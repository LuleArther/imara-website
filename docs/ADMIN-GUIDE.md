# Imara Health Bridge — Staff Guide

This guide is for the Imara team members who handle patient requests. You do not need any technical knowledge.

**Dashboard address:** `https://www.imaraafrika.org/admin/`

---

## 1. Signing in

- Use your own email and password. Never share an account, because the system records who did what.
- The first time you sign in, you will be asked to choose a new password: at least 10 characters, with letters and a number.
- You are signed out automatically after 30 minutes without activity.
- After 5 wrong passwords, sign-in is blocked for 15 minutes.
- Forgot your password? Ask an administrator to reset it (Staff users → Reset password).

## 2. Every morning (and through the day)

1. Open **Requests**.
2. If there is a **red urgent box** at the top, deal with those requests **first**.
3. Click **New** in the status tabs to see requests nobody has looked at yet.
4. Aim to contact every patient **within 24 hours on working days**, because that is the promise the website makes.

### Urgent requests

When a patient chooses "Urgent — need help today":

- The request is marked **URGENT** in red and stays at the top of the list.
- The email alert subject starts with **URGENT**.
- **Call the patient now.** If their symptoms sound severe (difficulty breathing, chest pain, heavy bleeding, fits, fainting, a very sick child), tell them to go to the nearest health facility immediately and not to wait for a referral.
- Imara is **not** an emergency service. Never let a patient wait for us when they need emergency care.

## 3. Handling a request, step by step

Click the reference number (for example **IMARA-0012**) to open it.

| Status | When to use it |
| --- | --- |
| **New request** | Received, nobody has reviewed it yet (set automatically) |
| **Under review** | You are looking at it or discussing it with the team |
| **Contacted** | You have spoken to the patient (or guardian) |
| **Referred** | The patient has been connected to a healthcare provider |
| **Linked to care** | The patient **confirmed** they actually received the service |
| **Follow-up required** | More contact is needed (no answer, needs another appointment…) |
| **Closed** | Finished, or closed with a reason (always choose a reason) |

On the right side of the request you can:

- **Assign** it to a team member.
- Record the **facility Imara recommends**. It can be different from what the patient chose.
- Record the **receiving institution**, **referral date** and **follow-up date**.
- Record **who approved the referral**. A qualified health professional must review every referral decision, and the system will not let you mark a request as *Referred* without this name.
- Record **whether the patient actually got care**, and the **follow-up outcome**.
- **Add a note** after every call (for example: "Called, no answer, will try again at 4pm").

Use the **Call patient** and **WhatsApp** buttons to contact the patient directly from your phone.

### Children

If the patient is under 18, the request shows the **parent or guardian** and their phone number. Speak with the parent or guardian about the child's care.

## 4. Privacy rules (very important)

- Only open the requests you need for your work. Every view is recorded in the audit log.
- **Never** copy a patient's health concern into an ordinary WhatsApp chat, a WhatsApp group, SMS or email. Share health details only with the provider who will see the patient, and only what they need.
- Do not take screenshots of patient records.
- Never assume anything about a patient's health. For example, choosing "HIV Testing" is a service request, not a status.
- Do not diagnose or recommend medicines. Imara links people to care; it does not treat.
- If a patient asks for their information to be deleted, tell an administrator, who can use **Anonymise request**.

## 5. Searching

Type in the search box at the top of **Requests**:

- a reference: `IMARA-0012` or just `12`
- a phone number: `0772 123 456`
- part of a name: `Akello`

You can also filter by service, urgent only, or "Assigned to me".

## 6. Other forms (Forms inbox)

Volunteer registrations, partnership applications, medical camp requests, contact messages and donation pledges arrive in **Forms inbox**. Open each one, contact the person, write a note, and set the status to *In progress* or *Done*.

**Donations:** the website records a **pledge only**. No money is taken on the website. Send the donor the **verified** payment details, and only mark it Done when the money has arrived and been checked against the mobile money or bank statement.

## 7. Reports

**Reports** shows weekly and monthly numbers: requests, referrals, patients linked to care, urgent requests, time to first contact, services, places and how people heard about Imara. Click **Print / PDF** for a report to share with partners, or **Download CSV** to open it in Excel. Reports contain **no names**.

## 8. Directory

**Directory** controls the public "Find a Hospital" and "Find a Specialist" pages. Only add doctors and facilities that have **agreed** to be listed. Changes show on the website immediately.

## 9. Administrators only

- **Staff users:** add team members and give each one only the role they need:
  - *Patient coordinator*: handles requests, forms, directory and reports
  - *Health professional*: reviews requests and approves referrals
  - *Viewer*: sees reports only, with no names or phone numbers
  - *Administrator*: everything
  When someone leaves Imara, **switch off** their account the same day.
- **Settings & data:** update the public impact numbers, set how long personal details are kept, run the clean-up, and download a backup.
- **Audit log:** see who viewed or changed each record.
- **Notifications:** check that email and WhatsApp alerts are being delivered. If anything shows **failed**, fix it quickly so no request is missed.

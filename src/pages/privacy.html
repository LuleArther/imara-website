Our promise

When you ask Imara for help, you trust us with personal and health information. We collect only what we need to link you to care, keep it confidential, and share it only for the purposes you agree to — subject to applicable legal requirements. We follow Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019, the Data Protection and Privacy Regulations, 2021, and the Ministry of Health's guidance on the confidentiality of health information.

1. Who we are

Imara Health Initiative AFRIKA (“Imara”, “we”) is a youth-led non-profit health organisation based in Gulu City, Northern Uganda. We run Imara Health Bridge Gulu, a free patient navigation and referral service. We decide how your information is used, so we are responsible for protecting it.

Imara is not an emergency, ambulance, diagnosis or treatment service. We do not diagnose illness or prescribe medicine.

Please do not include unnecessary personal or medical details in free-text boxes. Choosing a service such as “HIV Testing” only tells us which service you want; we never assume anything about your health status.

3. Why we collect it

  • To understand what care you need and contact you about it.
  • To link you to a suitable doctor, specialist, hospital, laboratory or Village Health Team member, and to follow up on whether you received care.
  • To keep you safe — for example, by flagging urgent requests for immediate review by a person.
  • To produce anonymous reports (numbers only) about our work for our team, partners and funders.
  • To respond to volunteers, partners, communities and donors.

A qualified health professional reviews referral decisions. We never make automatic medical decisions about you.

4. Your consent

We ask for two separate choices on the Get Care form:

  • Care coordination (required): permission to contact you and to share the information needed with a healthcare provider to arrange your care. We cannot help you without it.
  • WhatsApp health updates (optional): permission to send you general health information and follow-up messages. You can say no and still receive our help.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us. This will not affect anything we did before you withdrew it.

5. Children

If the patient is under 18, a parent or legal guardian (or someone with their permission) must give their contact details and consent before we can help. We will usually speak with the parent or guardian about the child's care.

6. Who sees your information

  • Imara staff and trained volunteers who handle your request. Each person only gets the access their role needs, and every time someone opens or changes a patient record it is recorded.
  • The healthcare provider we link you to — only the information they need to see you.
  • Service providers that help us run the website, email and the official WhatsApp Business Platform, under confidentiality obligations. Our staff alerts do not include your health details.
  • Authorities, only where the law requires it.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising. Reports we share with partners and funders contain numbers only, never names.

7. How we keep it safe

  • The whole website uses a secure, encrypted connection (HTTPS).
  • Names, phone numbers and health descriptions are encrypted in our database.
  • Staff sign in with personal accounts and role-based access. Accounts lock after repeated wrong passwords.
  • An audit log records who viewed or changed each patient record.
  • We keep secure backups and protect our forms against spam and automated attacks.

8. How long we keep it

We keep personal details for as long as we need them to coordinate and follow up on your care, and then for a limited period (currently up to 24 months after your request is completed) so we can answer questions about it. After that, we remove your personal details and keep only anonymous numbers for reporting. You can ask us to delete your details sooner — see below.

9. Your rights

Under the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019, you have the right to:

  • be told what information we hold about you and get a copy;
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your information, or stop using it, where the law allows;
  • object to how we use it, and withdraw consent at any time;
  • complain if you think we have not handled your information properly.

To use any of these rights, contact us with your request reference number if you have one. We may need to confirm who you are before we act.

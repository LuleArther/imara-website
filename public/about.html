Who We Are

About Us

A youth-led African health organisation working for health equity in communities shaped by conflict, displacement, poverty and weak health systems.

Headquartered in Gulu City, Northern Uganda

Who we are

Imara Health Initiative AFRIKA was founded by Lamtoo Clifton. We are young people who believe that where you were born, or how much money you have, should not decide whether you can see a doctor.

We run free medical camps, community outreaches, health education and preventive services for underserved and hard-to-reach communities. We work in partnership with communities, universities, government and development organisations.

Our centrepiece, Imara Health Bridge Gulu, helps patients find the right doctor, specialist, hospital or laboratory — and follows up to make sure they actually got care.

Our vision

A healthier Africa where everyone, regardless of circumstance, has access to quality healthcare.

Our mission

To empower underserved communities to drive better health outcomes by connecting patients to specialists, communities to healthcare institutions, and youth voices to health policy — through outreaches, digital health, podcasts, advocacy and youth leadership.

Our Three Pillars

Access, Voice and Leadership — three ways we build
strong communities and strong health.

1. Access

Imara Health Bridge Gulu

Health camps and outreaches. We connect patients to doctors, specialists, hospitals and laboratories.

2. Voice

Health Policy, Advocacy & Podcasts

Policy briefs, the Imara Voices Podcast and young people in health governance.

3. Leadership

Next Generation of Health Leaders

Training medical students, nurses and youth advocates in public health, leadership and diplomacy.

Bring a Camp to Your Community

Imara brings volunteer doctors, nurses and students to communities that find it hard to reach care. Here is how it works.

1. You send a request

Tell us about your community and its health needs

2. We call you

To understand the need, venue and local leaders

3. We plan together

Dates, services and volunteers — depending on resources

4. Camp day & follow-up

Screening, health talks and referrals through Imara Health Bridge

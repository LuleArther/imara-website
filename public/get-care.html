How it works

Imara is a navigation and referral service. We do not diagnose or treat — we connect you to people who can.

  • 1. Tell us what you need using this form.
  • 2. We review it. A qualified health professional checks every referral.
  • 3. We call you within 24 hours on working days.
  • 4. We link you to a doctor, specialist, hospital or lab.
  • 5. We follow up to make sure you got the care.
